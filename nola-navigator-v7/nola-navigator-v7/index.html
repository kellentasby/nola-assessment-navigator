    There are no tax brackets in Orleans: every home pays the same rate, about 132 mills ($132 per $1,000 of taxable value). S2 Bills differ by area because home values differ and because homestead takes $7,500 off the taxed value of homes owners live in. Colors show the median estimated 2026 tax for homes in each ZIP (from the 2026 roll, values as of Jan 1, 2023). S21 Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    About 132 mills ($132 per $1,000). Due Jan 31 S3

    Police and fire taxes (11.51 mills) are figured before the homestead break, so the math is: $22,500 × 120.48 ÷ 1,000 + $30,000 × 11.51 ÷ 1,000 = $3,056.10. Without homestead (like a rental) it would be about $3,960.
