Words to know

Market value
What the Assessor estimates your property would sell for. S1
Assessed value
10% of market value for homes and land. Taxes start from this number. S1
Homestead exemption
Takes $7,500 off the assessed value of the home you live in. S2
Mill
$1 of tax for every $1,000 of taxable value. S2
Board of Review
The City Council group that hears value appeals. S1
